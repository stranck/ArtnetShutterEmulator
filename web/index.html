Problems

    Edit selected

    Empty fields are left unchanged. Row k of the selection gets value + k × step, crossing into the next universe after .512 (512 = one universe).

    Duplicate selected

    The copies are inserted after the selection, with every source and destination moved by the offset. Names ending in a number keep counting. The offset is pre-filled with the footprint of the selection.

    Channel format

    universe.address, e.g. 1.001 or 17.120. Universes count from 1: universe 1 is Art-Net 0·0·0, universe 17 is 0·1·0 (the Inputs & outputs tab shows the number of each input). Destinations: separate with spaces or commas, ranges with - or thru (1.002-1.004).
